\label{groups-clubs-teams-and-program-societies}
\subsection{Groups}
\label{groups-definition}
\begin{enumerate}
  \item 
   A Group is a Club, Team, or Program Society 
   \begin{enumerate}
    \item  
     An Affiliate is not a Group.
   \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\label{program-societies}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item

  New Program Societies must put forth a motion for recognition by the Society at a General Meeting.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Ratification Process}
\label{ratification-process}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  To become an Affiliate, you must email the Director of Clubs your intent to become an Affiliate.
 \item
  After meeting with the Director of Clubs, all intending Affiliates must put forth a motion for affiliation at a Council Meeting.
 \item    
  The Director of Clubs is responsible for assisting Affiliates before, and after the Council Affiliation process regardless of result. 
 \item 
  To become a Group, you must be an Affiliate for at least one year, then inform the Director of Clubs of intent to apply to be a Group.
 \item
  Each Affiliate intending to become a Group must present a motion to Council. Upon a majority vote from Council, the Affiliate must then submit a motion for ratification at the Winter Semester General Meeting (See MES Bylaws Section \ref{general-meetings-sagm}).

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    On a majority vote at the General Meeting, the Affiliate will be ratified as a Group recognized by the Society.
   \item
    Motions may be put forth at the first General Meeting in a given academic year only by direct permission from the Director of Clubs.
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  Long-established groups may be able to bypass the affiliation stage and become directly ratified as a Group at the discretion of the Council.

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    This follows the same ratification process as Affiliates, wherein Council will motion whether to send the group to the Winter Semester General Meeting for ratification.
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

\subsection{De-Ratification Process}
\label{de-ratification-process}
If a Group is not performing or achieving certain standards, they may be de-ratified.

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The de-ratification process will occur at the Winter Semester General Meeting.
 \item
  The Director of Clubs shall be responsible for compiling a list of Groups that shall undergo the de-ratification process. Possible reasons for de-ratification include but are not limited to: 

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Group does not present at either General Meeting in that year, nor explains their absence to Council
   \item
    Group does not attend ClubsFest, or any outreach events during the academic year
   \item
    Group does not host events or maintain any social media presence during the academic year
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  The Director of Clubs will be responsible for communicating with Groups that may be facing de-ratification.
 \item
  The Director of Clubs will present to Council for approval all Groups that shall under-go the de-ratification process.
 \item    
  De-ratified Groups are not recognized by the Society. 
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{De-Affiliation Process} 
\label{de-affiliation-process} 
\begin{enumerate}
  \item 
   The de-affiliation process will occur at Council.
  \item   
   The Director of Clubs shall be responsible for compiling a list of Affiliates that shall undergo the de-affiliation process. 
  \item    
   The Director of Clubs shall make a reasonable effort to contact the Affiliates a minimum of 1 week before the de-affiliation meeting is to take place.
  \item     
   De-affiliated student organizations are not recognized by the Society.  
\end{enumerate}
