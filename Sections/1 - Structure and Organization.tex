\subsection{Authority and Responsibility of the Executive}
\label{authority-and-responsibility-of-the-executive}
\subsubsection{Executive Authority}
\label{executive-authority}

The Executive has the following authority:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  To administer the day-to-day business and operations of the Society.
 \item
  To advise the Council on the effects of the Council's policy decisions.
 \item
  In case of an emergency situation where the Council is unable to meet, the Executive may make an interim decision, although any policy decisions shall be subject to ratification at the next Council meeting.
 \item
  Approve expenditures not exceeding \$500, unless the expenditure is coming from the Executive Operations account.

\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Executive Responsibility}
\label{executive-responsibility}
The Executive shall have the following responsibility:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Review the budget prepared by the VP Finance for final recommendations to the Council.
 \item
  Form ad-hoc committees when required. 
 \item
  Provide accountability and transparency on relevant actions at Council, and to the general membership at each General Meeting.
 \item
  Initiate and encourage new events and initiatives. 
 \item
  Supervise appointed positions to provide advice or assistance.
 \item
  Liaise with other members or groups within the university community.
 \item
  Promote professionalism within the Society.
 \item
  Take a proactive role in the long term goal setting of the Society.
 \item
  Ensure effective transition and continuity between outgoing and incoming Executives, through documentation and orientation.
 \item
  Create a positive and effective working environment.
 \item
  Each hold at least 2 hours of scheduled office hours weekly.

\end{enumerate}

   \item

   \item
    Computer Science Society (CSS) 
   \item
    Electrical and Computer Engineering Society (ECES) 
   \item
    Engineering and Society Students' Association (ESSA)
   \item
    Engineering Physics Society (EPS) 
   \item

    Materials Science and Engineering Society (MSES)
   \item

   \item
    McMaster Mechatronic Society (MMS)
   \item

   \item

 \item
  The responsibilities of the Representatives are:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Act as a liaison between the Council, their respective program society and student body. 
   \item
    To keep the Society informed of all program and program society issues and activities.
   \item
    Advertise all events and activities to their respective constituents by making class announcements, distributing posters from the Graphic Designers, distributing publications, and promoting the Society website.

   \item

   \item
    Organize their constituency's team(s) for MES Sports Tournaments, with the exception of Management and Society.
   \item
    Represent the MES at their respective program society's meetings.
   \item
    Report to the President.
  \end{enumerate}

\end{enumerate}

   \item

   \item
    Promote committees to Level I students.
   \item

   \item

  \end{enumerate}
\end{enumerate}

\subsubsection{Executive Transition Process}
\label{executive-transition-process}
\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  The incoming Executive shall plan and attend a retreat to make long term plans for the year ahead.
 \item
  The President is to organize, plan and document the retreat.
 \item
  The budget for this retreat shall be set for no more than \$2000, taken from the Executive Operations budget line.
 \item
  The incoming Executive shall prepare a presentation for the first Council meeting following the retreat, outlining what was accomplished on the retreat, what the budget was used for, and informing Council of the year's objectives and long-term goals.
 \item
  The outgoing Executive shall remain engaged with the incoming Executive over the summer months to impart institutional knowledge, share best practices, and facilitate an effective transition.
 \item
  The outgoing VP Finance shall oversee the year-end closing, and ensure the completion of the audit for the fiscal year under their purview.
\end{enumerate}
