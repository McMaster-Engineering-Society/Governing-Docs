
\subsubsection{Director of First Year Council}
\label{director-first-year-council}
The Director of First Year Council shall:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Work with the CRO to recruit and run first year elections in September.
 \item
  Assist members of the First Year Council with running events, promotions, and initiatives of their choice.
 \item
  Act as a liaison between the First Year Council and the MES, providing updates on major events such as SAGM, Fireball, and appointed position applications.
 \item
  Develop strategies to improve first year recruitment and retention for the MES.
 \item
  Report to the President.
 \item
  Be a position held by a maximum of one person.
\end{enumerate}
