
\subsubsection{Publications Editor}
\label{publications-editor}
The Publications Editor shall:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Oversee the organization, production, and editing of the following publications:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Frequency
   \item
    Plumbline
   \item
    Handbook
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  Chair the Editorial Review Committee (see MES Bylaws Section 4.4.5) to enforce and review Editorial Policy (see MES Bylaws Section 11.1.3).
 \item
  Coordinate with the Website Coordinator(s) to maintain and update online publications.
 \item
  Ensure all publications are reviewed by the President prior to publishing.
 \item
  Maintain a respectful relationship with the current advertising and printing agencies. 
 \item   
  Work with the Sustainability Coordinator(s) to ensure sustainable practices in the printing of our publications.
 \item
  Hold a seat on the Advertising Committee.
 \item
  Work with the Advertising Committee to actively search for more ways to promote MES publications to the McMaster Engineering student body.
 \item
  Report to the VPC.
 \item
  Supervises the following positions:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Handbook Editor(s)
   \item
    Frequency Editor(s)
   \item
    Plumbline Editor(s)
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  Be a position held by a maximum of one person.

\end{enumerate}
