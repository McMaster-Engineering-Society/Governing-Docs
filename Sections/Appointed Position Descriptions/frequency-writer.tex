\subsubsection{Frequency Writer(s)}
\label{frequency-writers} 
The Frequency Writer(s) shall: 

\begin{enumerate} 
  \item     
   Write articles for the Frequency. 
  \item    
   Ensure that the written content of the Frequency is capable of being easily understood by the McMaster Engineering undergraduate student body. 
  \item    
   Ensure that all written content is appropriate (see MES Bylaws 11.1.3) and relevant to the Frequency (see MES Bylaws 11.1.5). 
  \item    
   Report to the Frequency Editor(s) 
  \item    
   Be a position held by a number of individuals decided at the discretion of VPC, Publications Editor, and Frequency Editors. 
\end{enumerate}
