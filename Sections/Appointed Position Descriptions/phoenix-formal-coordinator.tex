
\subsubsection{Phoenix Formal Coordinator(s)}
\label{phoenix-formal-coordinators}
The Phoenix Formal Coordinator(s) shall:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Organize all aspects of the Phoenix Formal dinner, foremost:

  \begin{enumerate}
   \item
    Booking the reception hall
   \item
    Catering
   \item
    Selling tickets
   \item
    Decorations at dinner
   \item
    Arrange speakers list
   \item
    Invite appropriate faculty guests
  \end{enumerate}
 \item
  Coordinate with each program representative, program society, VPSL, Faculty, and University Health and Safety to plan, approve, and clean up all Graduation pranks to ensure only safe and appropriate pranks are created.
 \item
  Report to the VPSL.
 \item
  Be a position held by a maximum of three people.

\end{enumerate}
