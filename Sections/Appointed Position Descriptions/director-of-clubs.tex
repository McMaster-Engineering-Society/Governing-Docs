
\subsubsection{Director of Clubs}
\label{director-of-clubs}
The Director of Clubs shall:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Meet with MES Groups to support them for Affiliation, Ratification or for other reasons as needed. 
 \item    
  Provide MES Groups bookings for Hatch Junction and Atrium, JHE, and ABB Tower space. 
 \item    
  Collaborate with University Health and Safety and ensure all MES Groups abide by its guidelines. 
 \item
  Promote MES flagship by encouraging the use of MES branding amongst MES groups.
 \item
  Chair the Club Leaders Committee (see MES Bylaws Section \ref{club-leaders-committee}).
 \item
  Serve on the Hatch Operations Committee (Faculty Committee). 
 \item
  Organize Clubsfest to facilitate connections between students and clubs
 \item    
  Organize and update the MES Groups list and bylaws as needed. 
 \item    
  Report to Vice President, Internal.

\end{enumerate}
