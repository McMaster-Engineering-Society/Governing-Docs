\subsubsection{Website Coordinator(s)} 
\label{website-coordinators} 
The Website Coordinator(s) shall: 

\begin{enumerate} 
  \item  
   Oversee website logistics and updates, ensuring the organization and accessibility of MES records, in collaboration with the Infrastructure Technology Committee. 
  \item    
   Maintain the organization of information and streamline the UI interface of the MES website (macengsociety.ca) in accordance with the guidelines set out in MES Bylaws Section 11.2. 
  \item    
   Report to the VP Internal and Infrastructure Technology Manager(s). 
  \item    
   Be a position held by a maximum of three people. 
\end{enumerate}
