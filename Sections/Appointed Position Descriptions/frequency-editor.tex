
\subsubsection{Frequency Editor(s)}
\label{frequency-editors}
The Frequency Editor(s) shall:

\begin{enumerate}
 \item
  Produce a minimum of two issues of the Frequency per academic year (see MES Bylaws Section \ref{frequency}).
 \item
  Actively seek contributions.
 \item
  Ensure that an electronic copy of the Frequency will be available for download on the MES website, on or prior to the day of hardcopy release.
 \item     
  Hold a seat on the Editorial Review Committee. 
 \item    
  Report to the Publications Editor.
 \item
  Supervise the following positions: 
  \begin{enumerate} 
    \item    
     Frequency Designer(s) 
    \item     
     Frequency Writer(s)   
  \end{enumerate} 
 \item  
  Be a position held by a maximum of two people.

\end{enumerate}
