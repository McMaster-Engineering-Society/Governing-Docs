
\subsubsection{Director of Operations}
\label{director-of-operations}
The Director of Operations shall: 

\begin{enumerate} 
  \item   
   Supervise the care and usage of all MES Equipment Rental (see MES Bylaws Section 11.7). 
  \item    
   Be responsible for ensuring that MES Groups using the equipment rentals are aware of how to safely handle them. 
  \item    
   Ensure all MES Groups renting equipment rentals complete the appropriate forms and provide the appropriate deposits. 
  \item    
   Assist VPI and GHC Coordinator(s) in organizing space allocations for MES Clubs and Teams in the Gerald Hatch Centre. 
  \item     
   Maintain the MES Google Workspace and the email service and mailing lists. 
  \item    
   Take appropriate action to protect passwords for the MES mailing lists and any other MES passwords. 
  \item    
   Report to VPI. 
  \item    
   Assist in supervision of the following positions: 
  
  \begin{enumerate} 
   \item    
    DW Lounge Coordinator(s) 
   \item     
    Gerald Hatch Centre Student Coordinator(s)    
   \item    
    Trailer Coordinator(s)   
  \end{enumerate} 
\end{enumerate}
