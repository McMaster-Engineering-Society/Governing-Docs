\subsubsection{Frequency Designer(s)}
\label{frequency-designers}
The Frequency Designer(s) shall: 

\begin{enumerate} 
  \item    
   Design the graphics and overall layout of the Frequency. 
  \item    
   Ensure that the writtern content of the Frequency is properly displayed and easy to navigate and read. 
  \item     
   Ensure that all imagery used is appropriate (see MES Bylaws 11.1.3) and relevant to the related article. 
  \item     
   Report to the Frequency Editor(s). 
  \item     
   Be a position held by a number of individuals decided at the discretion of VPC, Publications Editor, and Frequency Editors. 
\end{enumerate}
