\subsubsection{Plumbline Writer(s)}
\label{plumbline-writers}
The Plumbline Writer(s) shall: 

\begin{enumerate} 
  \item    
   Write content for the Plumbline 
  \item     
   Ensure that the written content of the Plumbline is capable of being easily understood by the McMaster Engineering undergraduate student body. 
  \item    
   Ensure that all written content is appropriate (see MES Bylaws 11.1.3) and relevant to the Plumbline (see MES Bylaws 11.1.4). 
  \item    
   Report to the Plumbline Editor(s). 
  \item    
   Be a position held by a number of individuals decided at the discretion of VPC, Publications Editor, and Plumbline Editors. 
\end{enumerate}
